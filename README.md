# Uritect Inc.

**Creativity is what drives us.**

Uritect is a Canadian technology company working where architecture, construction and software meet. Our mission is to bridge the built world with modern technology, so that anyone can imagine a space, understand it, improve it and bring it to life.

We build around four ideas: **learning, designing, solving and sharing.** Each of our products answers one of them, and together they follow a project from the first spark of an idea to the day the work is done.

Uritect is based at the University of Toronto's Schwartz Reisman Innovation Campus and is supported by Pycap Inc. and the U of T Entrepreneurship community.
