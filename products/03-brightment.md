# Brightment

**More light. More safety. More value.**

Too many basements are dark, uncomfortable and, in an emergency, hard to leave. Brightment changes that with a well-designed egress window: the large, code-compliant window that brings daylight downstairs and gives everyone in the room a real way out. It is often the difference between a storage cellar and a bedroom, a rental suite or a family room.

Brightment pairs a free design app with a full installation service. You see the result first, then one team makes it happen.

## The Brightment app: see it before you dig

Photograph the basement window you have today and receive a realistic picture of what it could become, in your own space.

### Interior Design: *see your basement with real daylight*
Take the photo from inside the room. The furniture, floor and walls stay as they are; the wall opens into a full egress window with sunlight across the room. Choose:

- **Window style:** slider, casement, hopper, or fixed with casement;
- **Frame finish:** white, almond, black or woodgrain;
- **Light:** bright midday, soft morning or warm afternoon.

### Exterior Design: *see the window well and landscaping before you dig*
Take the photo from the yard. The siding, roofline and driveway stay untouched; only the basement opening and the ground around it change. Choose:

- **Window style** and **frame finish**, as above;
- **Window well:** corrugated galvanized, stacked stone, poured concrete or composite;
- **Landscaping:** minimal, planted border, rock garden or full landscape.

Add a note about anything else you would like to change, then generate.

### Your projects and account
Every concept is saved to **My Projects**, where you can zoom in, download and share it. Your account keeps your credits, profile and password in one place, and you can delete it at any time. Sign in with email, Google or Apple.

## The Brightment service: one team, start to finish

When you are ready, Brightment takes care of the whole job:

1. **Site assessment** to confirm the design in person, because egress requirements vary by property and province.
2. **Design and engineering** of the window and well.
3. **Permits**, applied for on your behalf.
4. **Excavation, foundation cutting and installation.**
5. **Window well and landscaping**, finished to match your concept.

Most single-window installations are completed in **one day** once the permit is in hand.
