# Archibi Find Pro

**Build your practice with Archibi.**

Every Archibi design ends with the same question: *who can build this?* Find Pro is where that question meets its answer. Architects, interior designers and contractors create a profile, show their best work, and are discovered by clients who already know what they want.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Made for** | Architects, interior designers, contractors and other building professionals |
| **Available on** | The web, at [pro.archibi.ca](https://pro.archibi.ca) |
| **Where clients see you** | The *Pros* tab and *Find a Pro* in the Archibi app |
| **Price** | Free to apply |

---

## What a professional can do

### Apply in one guided form
Tell your story once and let your work speak for you:

- your name, profession, contact email, phone and website;
- a profile photo, plus a banner and cover image that set the tone;
- the sections and categories you work in, and up to 12 tags that describe your expertise;
- your location, so nearby clients can find you, and the days you are available each week;
- your CV and any supporting documents;
- your projects, each with a title, a description and images;
- and an optional note for the review team.

Each application receives a case number, so you always know where it stands.

### Pass a quality review
Every application is read by the Archibi team before it goes live. You will see whether your profile is *pending review*, *accepted* or *needs changes*. If something is missing, update your application and send it back.

### Run your profile
Once accepted, your profile becomes your shop window inside Archibi:

- edit your details, images, expertise and documents at any time;
- add, edit or remove projects as your portfolio grows;
- manage your password, appearance and account from Settings.

---

## What clients see

Inside the Archibi app, clients browse professionals by section, category and speciality. Your profile shows who you are, what you do, the tags that define your work and a gallery of your projects. Clients can save you to their favourites, share your projects, and contact you with a single tap.

Archibi makes the introduction. Agreements, quotes and on-site work are arranged directly between you and your client.

---

## Getting started

1. Visit [pro.archibi.ca](https://pro.archibi.ca) and choose **Join as a professional**.
2. Complete the application and verify your email.
3. Wait for the team's review, then sign in to manage your profile.

Already a member? [Sign in](https://pro.archibi.ca/login).
