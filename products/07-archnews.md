# ArchNews

**The built world, one story at a time.**

Architecture shapes how we live, work and gather, yet its best stories are often scattered across trade journals and specialist sites. ArchNews brings them together. It is Uritect's publication for architecture, design, construction, cities and materials from around the world, written for professionals and curious readers alike.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Made for** | Architects, designers, builders, students, and anyone who cares about the spaces around them |
| **Available on** | The web, at [archnews.ca](https://archnews.ca) |
| **Price** | Free to read |
| **Status** | Alpha: the new site is being prepared for launch |

---

## What readers will find

- **Architecture and design:** notable projects, new ideas and the people behind them.
- **Construction:** methods, technology and the realities of building today.
- **Cities:** housing, planning and how communities grow.
- **Materials:** what our buildings are made of, and what they could be made of next.

## Part of a bigger conversation

ArchNews continues the writing Uritect has long shared on its own blog, from the psychology of home design and the value of interior design in residential construction to the role of prefabricated homes in Ontario's housing challenge.
