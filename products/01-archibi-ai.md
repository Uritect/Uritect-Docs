## The Design Studio

The heart of Archibi. Five tools, each one a photo or a sketch away.

### Interior
Reimagine any room: living rooms, kitchens, bedrooms, bathrooms, dining areas and home offices. Try new layouts, colours and moods while the room keeps its true proportions and light.

### Exterior
Give a house a new face. Test cladding, colours, doors, windows, lighting and landscaping from one photo of the front of the building. Ideal for curb appeal, listings and planning before you call a contractor.

### Landscape
Redesign the outdoors. Gardens, backyards, patios and terraces come to life with planting, seating, pathways, outdoor lighting and zones for dining, relaxing or play.

### Sketch to Render
Draw an idea by hand, choose a style, and watch it become a realistic architectural image. Made for architects, students and designers who think with a pencil first.

### Find Furniture
Love a piece you saw somewhere? Photograph it, or the whole room. Archibi recognises the sofas, tables, chairs, lighting and rugs in the picture and shows similar pieces you can actually buy, with prices and the store selling each one. The catalogue draws on more than 100 stores.

## Shaping the result

Every design starts with a few simple choices, and each one is optional beyond the basics.

- **Design style.** More than 75 styles, from timeless classics to the latest contemporary looks.
- **Design mode.**
  - *Beautiful* makes careful changes, staying close to the space's structure and character. Best for practical updates and redecorating.
  - *Creative* gives the AI freedom to propose something bold: new layouts, new moods, even furnishing an empty room.
- **Space type.** The kind of room, building or garden you are working on.
- **Colour palette.** For interiors, pick a ready-made palette of three harmonised colours.
- **Materials.** Choose finishes for walls, ceilings and floors inside, façades outside, and paths in the garden.
- **Your own words.** Describe anything else you want, or type your own value in place of a preset. When your words and a preset disagree, your words win.

## Your results

Each design opens in a full-screen viewer. From there you can:

- zoom in to study the details,
- download the image to your phone or share it straight away,
- generate another version with one tap,
- and jump to **Find a Pro** when you are ready to make it real.

Every design is saved automatically to **My Projects**, and your latest five appear on the Home screen.

---

## Idea Bank

A curated library of interior, exterior and landscape inspiration, chosen by the Archibi team. Browse by room type, style or mood, tap the bookmark to keep an idea in **Favourite Ideas**, and use it as a starting point for your own design or a reference for your professional.

## Find a Pro

When a picture is not enough, Archibi connects you with architects, interior designers and contractors. Browse professionals by section, category and speciality, read their story, explore the projects in their gallery, and contact them directly. Tap the heart to keep someone in **Favourite Professionals**.

Archibi makes the introduction. Agreements and on-site work are arranged between you and the professional you choose.

## Getting started

1. Download Archibi and create your free account.
2. Open the Design Studio and pick Interior, Exterior, Landscape, Sketch or Furniture.
3. Add a photo or a sketch, choose a style and a mode, and generate.
4. Save, share, shop the look, or find a professional to build it.

## Good to know

Archibi's images are concept visualisations. They help you decide and communicate; they are not construction drawings, and they may not reflect exact dimensions, materials or structural requirements.
