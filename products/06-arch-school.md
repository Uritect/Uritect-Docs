# Arch School

**Free design education for women.**

Good design should not be a privilege. Arch School is Uritect's non-profit education initiative, created to make architecture, interior design and spatial thinking open to everyone, with a special focus on women and other groups who are underrepresented in the field. Women hold founding and key roles at Uritect, and Arch School is how we pass that opportunity forward.

| | |

## Workshops

Arch School runs short, practical workshops under Uritect's **Empowering Women** programme, including free one-day courses in Toronto and live online sessions.

### Featured workshop: Basic Concepts of Interior Design
A two-hour, women-only online class that teaches how to see a room the way a designer does.

- **You will learn:** space planning and layout, balance and proportion, colour harmony, lighting, and choosing materials, and how to apply each of them to a real room with simple tools.
- **You will receive:** a structured lesson with visual examples, practical tips for your home or future projects, and a short class project.
- **You will earn:** a certificate when you complete the project.

Places are reserved through Eventbrite, linked from the Arch School page.
