# Utect 3D

**Simple 3D design for the built world.**

Some ideas need more than a picture; they need a space you can walk around. Utect 3D is a design engine that opens in your web browser and lets you shape a whole home, or a single room, in three dimensions. No software to install and no design experience required.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Made for** | Homeowners, first-time designers, and owners planning an office or a store |
| **Available on** | Any modern web browser, at [uritect.com/unity](https://uritect.com/unity) |
| **Price** | Free to try |
| **Status** | Alpha: open for early users and their feedback |
| **Website** | [uritect.com/utect3d](https://uritect.com/utect3d/) |

---

## What you can do

- **Design a complete home or a single room.** Lay out the space and see it from every angle.
- **Arrange furniture.** Place pieces, move them around and find the layout that works.
- **Choose materials.** Try finishes and surfaces until the space feels right.
- **Generate realistic renders.** Turn your 3D scene into a lifelike image to share.
- **Design with real products.** Visualise your space with furniture you can actually buy.
- **Plan workspaces and shops.** Shape offices and stores that are practical for the people who use them and inviting for customers.

## Working with partners

Utect 3D is also a doorway to building. Uritect works with partners who can turn a finished design into a real home, and we welcome prefab home manufacturers in Canada and North America who would like to collaborate.

## Help shape what comes next

Utect 3D is in Alpha, and early users have a real say in where it goes. Try it, then tell us what you think through the feedback form on the Utect 3D page. Every piece of feedback earns a complimentary coffee on us.

## Getting started

1. Open [uritect.com/unity](https://uritect.com/unity) in your browser.
2. Start a room or a home and begin arranging.
3. Share your feedback from [uritect.com/utect3d](https://uritect.com/utect3d/).
